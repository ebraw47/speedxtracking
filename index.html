    In Transit, Out for Delivery, Delivered, Pending, or Exception — know what each scan usually means.

    SpeedX tracking status meanings explained

    What In Transit, Out for Delivery, Delivered, Pending, Exception, and Customs hold mean.

    ● In Transit

    Package is moving between hubs in the delivery network.

    ● Out for Delivery

    On a local vehicle — usually arrives the same day.

    ● Delivered

    Marked delivered. Check porch, lobby, mailbox, neighbors.

    ● Pending

    Label created / info received, not fully processed yet.

    ● Exception

    Delay or delivery issue — contact the seller with the TN.

    ● Customs hold

    Common on China→US marketplace orders; often clears in days.

    Tip: Marketplace shoppers (Shein, Temu, TikTok Shop) should contact the store first for claims, reroutes, or missing parcels — they hold the shipping contract with the carrier.

    Where is my SpeedX delivery?

    You can track your package by entering your SPX or SX tracking number on the SpeedX Tracking Portal (use the tracking box at the top of this page).

    How to find your package status

    • Check your number: Locate your SPX or SX tracking number from your retailer confirmation email (such as Shein, Temu, Amazon, or TikTok Shop).
    • Allow initial scanning time: If your status says “Shipping Label Created,” SpeedX has the label info but has not yet received the physical package. Wait 24 to 48 hours for the first scan.
    • Out for Delivery: If your status says “Out for Delivery,” the driver has your package and aims to deliver it by 10:00 PM local time. SpeedX delivers 7 days a week, including weekends.

    Contact SpeedX support

    If your package shows no movement for more than 3 business days or you need direct help:

    • Email: speedxops@speedx.io
    • Online support: Use the live chatbot on the SpeedX Customer Service page.

    Which company delivers for SpeedX?

    SpeedX does not own a traditional fleet of delivery trucks; instead, it relies on independent contractors, gig workers, and regional carriers to handle last-mile package delivery.

    How SpeedX operations work

    • Independent contractors: SpeedX contracts local drivers who use personal vehicles and log into the SpeedX Driver App to pick up and drop off packages from local warehouses and sort centers.
    • Regional & local partners: For areas outside its core urban coverage, SpeedX partners with local delivery networks, regional providers, and sometimes the U.S. Postal Service (USPS) for rural drop-offs.

    Companies that use SpeedX for deliveries

    SpeedX itself acts as a third-party last-mile delivery carrier rather than delivering for a single traditional parcel company. Instead, major e-commerce platforms and retailers hire SpeedX to ship their customer orders. These companies include:

    • Shein (often originating from international airfreight shipments)
    • Temu
    • TikTok Shop
    • Amazon (for specific small or international-origin parcels)
    • Vinted
    How many days does it take for SpeedX to deliver?

    Most SpeedX deliveries inside the United States take 2 to 7 business days once the package is physically scanned at a U.S. facility.

    Delivery breakdown and timelines

    • U.S. domestic transit: Generally takes 2 to 7 business days, with expedited options like next-day or 2-day delivery available depending on the retailer’s chosen service level.
    • International orders (e.g., from China): Total time often ranges from 10 to 15 days (or longer) because this includes overseas transit and customs clearance before SpeedX receives the package locally.
    • “Shipping Label Created” phase: If your tracking only shows a label has been created, SpeedX has not yet physically received the package from the seller. This initial handoff can add several days of waiting before movement begins.

    Delivery schedule and hours

    • Days of the week: SpeedX delivers 7 days a week in select areas, operating Monday through Sunday.
    • Delivery windows: Deliveries typically happen between 6:00 AM and 10:00 PM local time (though some weekend or regional routes vary).
    • Live tracking & updates: You can monitor real-time scan events and proof-of-delivery photos on the SpeedX official website or via the tracking portal on this page.
    How many times will SpeedX attempt delivery?

    SpeedX does not explicitly state a rigid, universal maximum number of delivery attempts in its official policy, though standard procedure typically involves a second attempt or triggers a return-to-sender (RTS) workflow after multiple failed tries or unresolved address issues.

    • First attempt: SpeedX aims to make the first delivery attempt on the next business day after receiving the package, with windows generally running from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM local time.
    • Failed attempts / “No Access”: If a delivery fails due to reasons like gate codes, missing unit numbers, or a closed business, the status often updates to “Attempted Delivery” or “Undeliverable.”
    • Redelivery & escalation: Escalate or contact support immediately after the second failed attempt before the package gets sent back to the sorting facility or merchant.

    How to contact support

    Because SpeedX primarily operates through local independent contractors and an AI chatbot named Miles, you can reach out via SpeedX Customer Support (hotline / email) or contact the merchant or marketplace (such as Shein, Temu, or Vinted) directly to request a redelivery or refund.

    Does SpeedX work on the weekends?

    Yes, SpeedX delivers on the weekends, operating seven days a week.

    Weekend delivery hours

    • Saturdays: Deliveries occur regularly from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM local time across all service areas.
    • Sundays: Deliveries are available from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM local time, though Sunday service is route-dependent and less consistent than Saturday or weekday delivery.

    What to expect

    • Support hours: While package delivery runs through the weekend, corporate customer support or specific technical help lines may be closed or limited on Saturdays and Sundays. (Hotline hours still list Sat–Sun 11 AM – 8 PM EST when staffed.)
    • Reliability: Weekend deliveries can occasionally face local route overflows or unexpected delivery exceptions.

    Why hasn't my SpeedX package arrived?

    Your SpeedX package may be delayed due to several common carrier issues:

    • Inaccessible addresses or blocked routes: Couriers often mark packages as undeliverable or delayed if they cannot access a gate, apartment building, or blocked street.
    • Infrequent drops or scans: Some drop-off locations or transit hubs only process and scan packages every 4 to 5 days, leaving tracking numbers stuck on “In Transit.”
    • Driver backlogs or late marking: Independent drivers operating from regional warehouses may run out of time on their routes and log a delay late at night.
    • Misdeliveries or faked scans: Drivers have occasionally delivered packages to wrong addresses, left them unsecured, or marked them delivered without dropping them off.

    What you can do

    • Check your tracking: Review your tracking number (starting with SPX) on the SpeedX tracking portal at the top of this page.
    • Contact SpeedX support: Call 1-949-667-6769 (Mon–Fri 9 AM–9 PM, Sat–Sun 11 AM–8 PM EST), email speedxops@speedx.io, or chat with their AI bot Miles on speedx.io.
    • Contact the merchant: If the package is lost, damaged, or severely delayed, file a claim or request a refund or replacement directly through the store or platform you bought it from (such as Temu or TikTok Shop), as SpeedX often directs end-users back to the merchant for resolution.

    Why does SpeedX take long to deliver?

    SpeedX takes a long time primarily because of high order volumes, a gig-economy delivery model, and sparse drop-off or sorting schedules.

    Main causes of SpeedX delays

    • Massive order volume: SpeedX handles over a million daily parcels for major fast-fashion and e-commerce platforms like Shein, Temu, and Vinted, leading to frequent sorting backlogs at regional hubs.
    • Gig-economy drivers: The company relies heavily on independent contractors using personal vehicles. These drivers are paid per package and may skip, delay, or return deliveries if an address is hard to navigate, lacks a gate code, or takes longer than a few minutes.
    • False or premature “Failed Delivery” scans: Drivers who run out of time on their routes sometimes mark packages as “failed delivery” or issue fake proof-of-delivery scans late at night to avoid route penalties.
    • Infrequent drop-off pickups: Some local drop-off points or seller handoff locations only collect packages every 4 to 5 days, causing tracking to stall right after drop-off.
    • Inconvenient customer support: Users report long hold times and unhelpful automated responses when trying to resolve stuck shipments through SpeedX Customer Service and Support.
