Ship With Us

Cross-border shipping built for U.S. ecommerce

Reaching U.S. customers should not feel harder than domestic shipping. This guide walks through customs clearance, hub injection, and last-mile delivery for cross-border retailers and logistics teams.

Why cross-border last mile fails without a plan

International parcels stall at customs, sit in unsorted inbound piles, or lose tracking between the gateway and the local courier. A connected cross-border flow clears freight, injects into a U.S. delivery network, and keeps shoppers updated until the doorstep.

  • Integrated brokerage (including Type 01 & T11 style clearance)
  • Strategic airport hubs for inbound volume
  • Secure retrieval, sortation, and last-mile injection
  • Transparent rates and end-to-end tracking with POD photos

How it works

01 · Freight arrival & customs clearance

Inbound volume lands at major airport hubs and moves through compliance-focused clearance so parcels are not stuck in limbo.

02 · Secure transport & sortation

Cleared shipments are retrieved, sorted, and injected into the domestic last-mile network for efficient outbound routes.

03 · Next-day & two-day delivery

Optimized routing targets strong on-time performance so international orders still feel fast to the end customer.
