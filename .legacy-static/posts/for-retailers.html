Ship With Us

Last-mile delivery for retailers, marketplaces & DTC

From order to doorstep, fast delivery is how shoppers judge your brand. This guide covers how ecommerce retailers can use a SpeedX-style last-mile network for next-day and two-day shipping, clear rates, and visible tracking.

Why retailers choose a dedicated last-mile partner

Online shoppers expect speed and proof that their order is moving. A focused last-mile partner helps you hit high on-time targets (often around 95%), keep peak season stable, and avoid locking into a single carrier when volumes spike.

  • Scalable next-day and second-day options that grow with volume
  • Transparent parcel pricing without surprise surcharge stacks
  • Carrier diversification so peak demand does not stall checkout
  • Live tracking, SMS updates, and photo proof of delivery

Built for marketplaces and DTC brands

Ecommerce retailers & marketplaces need flexible capacity, peak-season support, and predictable handoffs from fulfillment into the delivery network.

DTC brands need a polished delivery experience: customized service levels, end-to-end visibility, and support that matches your brand promise after the sale.

Cross-border retailers selling into the U.S. can combine customs clearance, handling, and injection into the same last-mile network so international orders feel as smooth as domestic ones. See our cross-border shipping guide.
